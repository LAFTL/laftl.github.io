Case study

Email

A Google Apps Script project that saves email bodies as PDFs and preserves their attachments in Google Drive. It combines scheduled processing, execution locking and checks for existing files.

JavaScriptGoogle Apps ScriptGoogle Drive

The problem

Email correspondence and attachments often need to be kept together in an accessible record. Manual filing involves repeated steps and makes consistent organisation harder.

My contribution

I built the email-to-Drive workflow and its scheduling support. The implementation includes locking to coordinate runs and file checks to reduce repeated saves.

What the source demonstrates

  • Email content converted into a PDF record
  • Attachments preserved alongside the record
  • Scheduled execution and concurrency controls
  • Existing-file checks and a test suite

These are implementation features. No measured time savings or production reliability claim is made.
