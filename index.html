Why Stryker

Your ambulance stretchers are the reason I’m applying today. I’ve used them, I’ve been on them, and I’ve watched them get tinkered with. I’ve saved patients on them and had to watch patients pass away on them. They’re amazing pieces of technology. They save backs, help with mobility, and move people from point to point.

I want to be part of that. Plus, the Stryker sales representative I met at a career expo before I started nursing was so passionate about their products.

Capital equipment is for me. If I have to study or travel to meet my targets, so be it.
